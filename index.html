I'm Adam — an experienced Systems Administrator specializing in GIS and spatial analysis. My background includes designing and supporting enterprise infrastructure across VMware environments, managing backups and disaster recovery, and administering Windows, Linux, and cloud-based systems. Recently, I’ve been focused on building geospatial workflows using ArcGIS Pro, ModelBuilder, and spatial analysis techniques. This includes working with multiple data layers, performing spatial joins, standardizing attributes, and developing repeatable processes for data preparation and analysis. My combined experience allows me to approach GIS projects with a strong understanding of data integrity, system performance, and scalable solutions. Outside of work, I spend most of my time enjoying the outdoors with my dogs, whether that’s camping, off-roading, or working on and improving my property. I value the balance between technical problem-solving and time spent outside, and I’m naturally drawn to environments where I can explore, build, and improve things. That same mindset carries into my professional work, where I enjoy creating efficient systems, refining workflows, and continuously learning new tools and technologies.
